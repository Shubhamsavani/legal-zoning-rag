# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-43: Height and Setback Requirements in R6 Through R12 Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 4/30/2024  
**Retrieved:** March 2026

---

### 23-431: Street Wall Location Requirements

In R6A, R6B, R7A, R7B, R7D, R7X, R8A, R8B, R8X, R9A, R9D, R9X, R10A, and R10X Districts, the **street wall** of any **building** shall be located as follows:

(a) Within 8 feet of the **street line**, except as otherwise provided in paragraphs (b) through (d) of this Section;

(b) Where an existing building on the same **zoning lot** has a **street wall** located further from the **street line** than required by paragraph (a), the new **street wall** shall be located no further from the **street line** than such existing **street wall**;

(c) At a **street line** where the **building** contains ground floor retail or other non-residential **uses**, the **street wall** shall be located at the **street line**, or within 2 feet thereof, for a minimum of 70 percent of the **street** frontage.

---

### 23-432: Height and Setback Requirements

In R6 through R12 Districts, **buildings** shall comply with a base height and setback envelope before a tower or upper portion may rise to the maximum permitted height.

**Base heights** (maximum before setback is required):

| District | Min Base Height | Max Base Height |
|---|---|---|
| R6A | 25 ft | 45 ft |
| R6B | 25 ft | 35 ft |
| R7A | 40 ft | 65 ft |
| R7B | 30 ft | 45 ft |
| R7X | 60 ft | 85 ft |
| R8A | 60 ft | 85 ft |
| R8B | 40 ft | 65 ft |
| R8X | 60 ft | 105 ft |
| R9A | 60 ft | 85 ft |
| R9X | 60 ft | 105 ft |
| R10A | 60 ft | 105 ft |

After the base height is reached, a setback of at least 10 feet from the **street wall** shall be provided before the **building** may rise to its maximum permitted height.

In standard R6, R7, R8, R9, and R10 Districts (non-contextual), the sky exposure plane regulations of Section 23-73 apply in lieu of this table.

---

### 23-433: Standard Setback Regulations

In R6A, R6B, R7A, R7B, R7D, R7X, R8A, R8B, R8X, R9A, R9D, R9X, R10A, and R10X Districts, above the maximum base height, a setback of at least 10 feet from the **street wall** below shall be provided at all **street** frontages before the **building** may rise to the maximum permitted height for the district.

Where a **building** does not front on a **street**, setback requirements shall be determined by the provisions for **rear yards** and **side yards** applicable to the district.
