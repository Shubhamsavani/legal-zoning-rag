# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-34: Rear Yard and Rear Yard Equivalent Requirements
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 5/12/2021  
**Retrieved:** March 2026

---

### 23-342: Rear Yard Requirements

**Applicable Districts:** R1 through R10

In all districts, as indicated, a **rear yard** shall be provided at every required **rear lot line** of a **zoning lot**, except as otherwise provided in Sections 23-341 (Permitted obstructions in required rear yards or rear yard equivalents) and 23-344 (Additional rear yard modifications).

The minimum required **rear yard** depth shall be:

| District | Minimum Rear Yard Depth |
|---|---|
| R1 through R5 | 30 feet |
| R6 through R10 | 30 feet |

For corner lots in any Residence District, no rear yard shall be required.

For through lots in any Residence District, each **street** frontage shall be subject to front yard requirements and no rear yard shall be required; however, where a through lot is also a corner lot, the lot shall be subject to both front yard and corner lot regulations.

In R1 through R5 Districts, for lots less than 95 feet in depth, the **rear yard** shall not be less than 20 feet.

**Note:** Permitted obstructions that may be placed in required rear yards — including certain accessory structures, mechanical equipment, and open recreational space elements — are governed by Section 23-341, not this Section. A reader relying solely on Section 23-342 without consulting Section 23-341 will have an incomplete picture of what is permissible in the rear yard.

---

### 23-343: Rear Yard Equivalent Requirements

Where a **zoning lot** does not have a **rear lot line** as defined in this Resolution, a **rear yard equivalent** shall be provided. The **rear yard equivalent** shall comply with the same minimum depth requirements as specified in Section 23-342.

---

### 23-344: Additional Rear Yard Modifications

The **rear yard** requirement may be modified under certain conditions, including:

(a) Where the **Board of Standards and Appeals** grants a variance pursuant to Article VII.

(b) For developments on **zoning lots** that are predominantly occupied by existing buildings, certain alternative rear yard depths may apply as set forth in Section 23-711 (Predominantly Built-up Areas).

(c) In the Special Purpose Districts established under Articles VIII through XIV, alternative rear yard requirements may supersede those set forth in this Section; the applicable Special Purpose District regulations shall control.
