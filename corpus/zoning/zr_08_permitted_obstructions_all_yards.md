# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-311: Permitted Obstructions in All Yards, Courts and Open Areas
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 11/10/2022  
**Retrieved:** March 2026

---

In all districts, the following shall not be considered obstructions and may penetrate any **yard**, **court**, or other required open area:

(a) **Awnings and canopies**, provided that they are not more than 4 feet deep measured from the exterior face of the building wall, and provided that they do not extend beyond 18 inches from any **lot line**.

(b) **Belt courses, sills, headers, pilasters, cornices, eaves** or **gutters**, provided that such features do not extend more than 2 feet into any **yard**.

(c) **Open fire escapes**, provided that they do not extend more than 3.5 feet into any **yard** and are not located within 15 feet of any **street** or **lot line**, except in the case of lots less than 50 feet in width.

(d) **Handicapped ramps and access features** required by the New York City Building Code or the Americans with Disabilities Act, provided that such ramps do not extend more than 4 feet into any required **front yard**.

(e) **Planters, garden structures** (including pergolas, trellises, and lattice work not exceeding 8 feet in height), provided such structures do not cover more than 25 percent of the open yard area.

(f) **Driveways and walkways** at grade, in any **side** or **rear yard**, providing access to a **street** or to an **accessory** off-street parking space.

(g) **Steps** (including stoops) leading to the entrance of a **building**, provided that they do not occupy more than 30 square feet of required **front yard** area.

(h) **Heating, ventilation and air conditioning equipment** (condensers and similar elements), provided that such equipment does not extend more than 2 feet into any **rear yard** and is not located within any **front yard**. Such equipment shall comply with the noise and screening provisions of Section 26-50.

**Note on scope:** This Section governs permitted obstructions in *all* yards. Additional permitted obstructions specific to rear yards are set forth in Section 23-341. Where a feature is listed in both sections, the more permissive provision applies.
