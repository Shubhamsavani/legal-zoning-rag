# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-32: Front Yard Requirements
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 6/3/2020  
**Retrieved:** March 2026

---

### 23-321: Basic Front Yard Requirements in R1 Through R5 Districts

In all R1 through R5 Districts, a **front yard** shall be provided for every **zoning lot** and shall be not less than the depth specified in the following table, except as otherwise provided in Sections 23-311 (Permitted obstructions in all yards, courts and open areas) and 23-312 (Additional permitted obstructions generally permitted in all yards).

| District | Minimum Front Yard Depth |
|---|---|
| R1 | 20 feet |
| R2 | 20 feet |
| R3 | 15 feet |
| R3A | 15 feet |
| R3X | 20 feet |
| R4 | 10 feet |
| R4A | 20 feet |
| R4B | 10 feet |
| R5 | 10 feet |
| R5A | 20 feet |
| R5B | 15 feet |
| R5D | 10 feet |

**Contextual front yard modification:** Where 40 percent or more of the lots within 150 feet on either side of the **zoning lot** and on the same side of the **street**, within the same **block**, are occupied by **buildings** with front yards of less than the depth required by this Section, the minimum required front yard depth may be the average of the two such front yards nearest to each side of the **zoning lot** in question, but in no event less than 8 feet.

**Planting requirement:** In all R1 through R5 Districts, at least 50 percent of the area of any required front yard shall be planted and maintained with **landscaped** ground cover or other plantings, pursuant to Section 23-613 (Front yard planting requirements).

---

### 23-322: Front Yard Requirements for R6 Through R12 Districts

In R6 through R12 Districts, no minimum **front yard** depth is required as a general rule; however, **street wall** location requirements and setback provisions set forth in Section 23-431 (Street wall location requirements) and Section 23-433 (Standard setback regulations) shall apply where indicated.
