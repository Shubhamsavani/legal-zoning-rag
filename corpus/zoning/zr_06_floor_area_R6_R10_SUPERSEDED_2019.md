# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-22: Floor Area Regulations for R6 Through R10 Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**⚠️ SUPERSEDED — This version reflects the text as of December 2019. This document is provided for historical reference. It does not reflect amendments adopted after December 31, 2019.**  
**Last Amended (as of this version):** 9/25/2019

---

In all R6 through R10 Districts, **floor area** shall not exceed the maximum **floor area ratio (FAR)** specified in the following table.

| District | Maximum FAR (Residential) | Maximum FAR (Community Facility) |
|---|---|---|
| R6 | 2.43 | 4.80 |
| R6A | 3.00 | 4.80 |
| R6B | 2.00 | 2.00 |
| R7 | 3.44 | 6.50 |
| R7A | 4.00 | 6.50 |
| R7B | 3.00 | 4.00 |
| R7D | 4.20 | 6.50 |
| R7X | 3.75 | 6.50 |
| R8 | 6.02 | 10.00 |
| R8A | 6.02 | 10.00 |
| R8B | 4.00 | 6.50 |
| R8X | 6.00 | 10.00 |
| R9 | 7.52 | 10.00 |
| R9A | 7.52 | 10.00 |
| R9D | 7.00 | 10.00 |
| R10 | 10.00 | 10.00 |
| R10A | 10.00 | 10.00 |

**Note:** R9D FAR was 7.00 under this version. It was subsequently increased to 9.00 by amendment. R9X and R10X designations were added in later amendments and do not appear in this document.

**Inclusionary Housing bonus:** In designated Inclusionary Housing areas, additional floor area is permitted for affordable housing per Section 23-90. The Mandatory Inclusionary Housing program (MIH), established under separate later amendments, introduced additional compliance pathways not reflected here.
