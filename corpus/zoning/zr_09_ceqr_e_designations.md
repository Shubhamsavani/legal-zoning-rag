## What is an (E) Designation?

An **(E) designation** is an environmental requirement placed on a **zoning lot** as part of a zoning map amendment or other CEQR action. The designation identifies a site that may have potential hazardous materials, air quality, or noise concerns that must be addressed before a building permit can be issued.

There are three types of (E) designations:
- **(E) Hazardous Materials** — the lot may contain soil or groundwater contamination requiring investigation and potentially remediation before development.
- **(E) Air Quality** — development on the lot must incorporate specific air quality mitigation measures (e.g., HVAC intake placement, building materials).
- **(E) Noise** — development must achieve specified interior noise attenuation levels.

A single lot may carry multiple (E) designation types simultaneously.

---

## How to Use This Table

(E) designations are indexed by **CEQR Number** and **Tax Block and Lot** (BBL). The presence of a **Hazardous Materials (E)** designation does not necessarily mean contamination is present — it means an investigation is required. The outcome of such investigation may clear the designation or may require remediation, subject to review by the NYC Mayor's Office of Environmental Remediation (OER).

**Important:** (E) designations attach to the zoning lot as defined in this Resolution and run with the land. They are not automatically removed upon sale or change of ownership. Only formal OER sign-off following a Phase II ESA and any required remediation can lift the designation.
