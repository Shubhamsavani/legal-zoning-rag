# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-341: Permitted Obstructions in Required Rear Yards or Rear Yard Equivalents
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 11/10/2022  
**Retrieved:** March 2026

---

In all Residence Districts, the following shall not be considered obstructions when located in a required **rear yard** or **rear yard equivalent**:

(a) **Fences or walls** not exceeding six feet in height as measured from natural grade.

(b) **Arbors or trellises**.

(c) **Recreational and play equipment**, including children's play apparatus, sandboxes, swimming pools (above-ground), and similar structures, provided that no such structure shall exceed a height of six feet as measured from natural grade.

(d) **Statuary, flagpoles**, or similar ornamental features not exceeding 15 feet in height.

(e) **Solar energy systems**, including solar panels mounted on, or not more than 18 inches above, the roof of a structure located in the rear yard, provided such structure is itself a permitted obstruction.

(f) **Planting, including trees**, shrubbery, and ground cover.

(g) **Accessory buildings or other structures** not exceeding 10 feet in height as measured from natural grade, provided that the total **floor area** of all such structures does not exceed 80 square feet, and provided that such structures are set back at least four feet from the **rear lot line** and from each **side lot line**.

**Important limitation:** Air conditioning units, mechanical equipment serving the principal building, and emergency generators are NOT listed as permitted obstructions in rear yards under this Section. Such equipment shall comply with the screening requirements of Section 26-50. Where a conflict exists between this Section and any Special Purpose District regulation, the Special Purpose District regulation shall control (see Section 12-01(a): the particular shall control the general).
