# NYC Zoning Resolution — Article II, Chapter 3
## Section 23-22: Floor Area Regulations for R6 Through R12 Districts
**Source:** NYC Department of City Planning — zr.planning.nyc.gov  
**Last Amended:** 4/30/2024  
**Retrieved:** March 2026

---

In all R6 through R12 Districts, **floor area** shall be calculated as set forth in Section 12-10 (DEFINITIONS) and shall not exceed the maximum **floor area ratio (FAR)** specified in the following table.

| District | Maximum FAR (Residential) | Maximum FAR (Community Facility) |
|---|---|---|
| R6 | 2.43 | 4.80 |
| R6A | 3.00 | 4.80 |
| R6B | 2.00 | 2.00 |
| R7 | 3.44 | 6.50 |
| R7A | 4.00 | 6.50 |
| R7B | 3.00 | 4.00 |
| R7D | 4.20 | 6.50 |
| R7X | 3.75 | 6.50 |
| R8 | 6.02 | 10.00 |
| R8A | 6.02 | 10.00 |
| R8B | 4.00 | 6.50 |
| R8X | 6.00 | 10.00 |
| R9 | 7.52 | 10.00 |
| R9A | 7.52 | 10.00 |
| R9D | 9.00 | 10.00 |
| R9X | 8.00 | 10.00 |
| R10 | 10.00 | 10.00 |
| R10A | 10.00 | 10.00 |
| R10X | 10.00 | 10.00 |

**Inclusionary Housing bonus:** In designated Mandatory Inclusionary Housing (MIH) areas as listed in Appendix F, additional **floor area** may be available subject to the affordable housing requirements of Section 23-154 or 23-90. The MIH bonus does not apply in R6B or R7B Districts.

**Note on community facility uses:** The maximum FAR for **community facility** uses applies only to buildings used exclusively for such use, or to the community facility portion of a mixed-use building, as set forth in Section 24-01 (Applicability of Article II, Chapter 4). Calculations shall not simply add residential and community facility FAR caps together.
